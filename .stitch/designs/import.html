How to Export from MARG

1

Open MARG ERP

Log in to your MARG software dashboard.

2

Go to Reports

Navigate to the 'Reports' section in the top menu.

3

Select Export Type

Choose the data type you want to export (e.g., Medicine Master, Sales).

4

Choose Format

Select CSV or Excel as the export format.

5

Save & Upload

Save the file to your computer and upload it here.
